### Review notes

- Page 2: keep the mass term outside the overall `i hbar` factor, then multiply on the left by beta to obtain the covariant equation.
- Pages 3-4: the first momentum appears to be `sqrt(2) mc`; the final calculation and spinor instead use `sqrt(3) mc`. The appendix identifies the discrepancy and gives the general coefficients.
- The appendix distinguishes spinor normalization from spatial normalization, and lower Dirac-basis components from antiparticle amplitudes.
- The remaining additions cover currents, Lorentz covariance, chirality, massless spin-1/2 fields and the motivation for second quantization.
