Physics

Quantum Mechanics

A rigorous introduction to quantum mechanics and one of the books I've used while building my foundations in the subject.

Quantum Mechanics

Modern Quantum Mechanics

J. J. Sakurai & Jim Napolitano
A more modern and elegant treatment of quantum mechanics. Particularly useful once the basic formalism is already familiar.

Classical Mechanics

Classical Mechanics

John R. Taylor
A clear and approachable introduction to classical mechanics, from Newtonian mechanics to more advanced ideas.

Electromagnetism

An excellent introduction to electromagnetism with a balance of physical intuition, mathematics, and problems.
